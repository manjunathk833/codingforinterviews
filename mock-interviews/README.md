## 🧭 Workflow & Agent Usage

### 1. Start a New Interview Session
In Antigravity chat, type:
```text
/mock-interview start
```
The agent will:
- Detect the next session index (`interview-1/`, `interview-2/`, etc.).
- Research current market interview trends and calibrate questions against your background (Java, Python, Playwright, REST Assured, Microservices, Agentic AI, CI/CD).
- Generate `interviewquestions.md` with structured question prompts and answer blocks.

### 2. Answer the Questions
Open `mock-interviews/interview-N/interviewquestions.md` and type your technical answers in the designated `✍️ Candidate Answer` sections.

### 3. Evaluate & Generate Conceptual Mastery
Once you have written your answers, type:
```text
/mock-interview evaluate
```
The agent will analyze your responses and generate **two comprehensive documents** inside that session folder:
1. **`evaluation-and-feedback.md`**: Detailed rubric score (out of 100), gap analysis, missed edge cases, and Staff-level benchmark answers.
2. **`conceptual-mastery-guide.md`**: Exhaustive conceptual theory, intuitive mental models, internal mechanics, production blueprints (Java & Python), and MAANG interview gotchas.
